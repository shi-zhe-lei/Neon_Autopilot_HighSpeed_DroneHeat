# Rendering / 渲染

## 中文

保存程序化建模与物理光影。此层拥有材质、HDR、真实灯光和阴影合同，不拥有玩法权威。

rig 在道路池之前建立场景级 `scene.userData.neonTunnelLightingUniforms`，以三个稳定 uniform 公布实际环境光、已缓存 HDR、方向光的全局洞内/室外强度比例。道路/墙顶/肋架只读共享它们，把局部目标透射除以已有全局透射并限制不超过 `1`，避免洞内二次压暗，也不把室外表面提亮。关闭光影时比例复位 `1`，销毁仅归还自己仍持有的场景槽；无需逐帧重建材质或着色器。

物理光影合同 v13 修复 Three r160 对普通 `DataTexture` 忽略 `needsPMREMUpdate` 的缓存行为：仅当既有 `0.025` 量化环境签名改变时，rig 对自己独占的 HDR source 发送 `dispose` 生命周期事件，释放旧的 source/PMREM GPU 缓存，再用 `needsUpdate` 更新相同的 Float 存储；下一次实际绘制才生成新 PMREM。稳定环境、重复暂停绘制及关闭光影时不触发重新滤波。`environmentCacheInvalidationCount` 统计缓存失效次数，不等同于 GPU 实际构建次数。HDR 仍为 `64×32` Float、线性过滤及原辐射值，曝光、灯数、阴影分辨率和画质档位均保持既有规格。

隧道动态灯光的 `update({ dtSeconds })` 接收已排除暂停时间的实际帧秒数；`0` 冻结交接进度，省略时仅为旧调用兼容使用 `1/60` 秒。以原 `0.34 @ 60Hz` 为标尺，淡入采用等效指数响应，淡出采用每秒 `20.4` 的线性速率。换组必须让全部旧 edge/profile 灯光归零，再把该帧剩余时间用于新组淡入；30/60/120Hz 同一经过时间得到相同亮度，不再多等待由更新次数造成的空白帧。六盏聚光、其中两盏局部投影、真实 emitter 位置和完整静态基础照明保持不变。

`lighting.js` 仍是唯一全局灯光权威，并在 High 使用对比度更高的曝光/HDR/环境光配比与按设备回退的 `8K / 4K / 4K` 阴影。`postprocessing.js` 只在 High 分配 4× MSAA、深度 AO、显式发光 Bloom、单次 ACES Filmic 与弱暗角缓冲；MSAA 可用时合成 Shader 不再执行结果权重恒为零的 FXAA 采样，私有全覆盖缓冲也不再重复清屏。两项优化保持格式、采样数、Shader 有效数学与最终像素不变。切到 Low/Medium 会释放七个 RenderTarget 并回到直接渲染。

隧道灯具的供电与近距动态细节相互独立：山体廊道和地下隧道的灯罩/扩散片沿完整路线密排，所有已驻留灯具全程发亮；同一 authored station 还把有界静态照度写入既有道路、连续墙顶壳体以及肋架/灯壳实例属性，由 Low/Medium/High 的原批次着色器接收，因此镜头看得到的完整隧道始终有路面、墙、顶和结构基础照明，不新增 Mesh 或绘制组。结构壳、侧墙、肋架和道路的基础材质 `emissive` 仍为 `0`。只有 High 启用六盏复用聚光作为摄影机附近的弱 PBR 高光与阴影，其中两盏投影；Low/Medium 在读取 emitter 列表前直接短路，不校验、匹配或预热这些灯，也不会把零功率灯提交给渲染器。切入 High 会安排两个更新周期的局部阴影预热；从 High 降到 Low/Medium 会在同一次画质事务中灭掉全部聚光、清除普通/Film 身份与交接状态，并释放局部 `shadow.map`/`mapPass`。High 普通视角要求玩家 edge/profile 精确匹配，Film 则从摄影机附近灯具选定唯一最近的 authored edge/profile 组，交叉路线不能混灯。旧 `144m` 硬阈值改为 `24m` 平滑尾带；第七盏进入或 Film 切到另一 edge/profile 时，旧真实灯位都先在原六槽内降至近零，槽位才绑定新组的真实 emitter 并从零淡入。`constant-on` 供电不再乘玩家接近、洞门距离或遮蔽开关；缺少精确身份或旧记录已卸载时动态细节安全关闭，且不生成跟随玩家/镜头的伪 emitter。洞门仍连续衰减户外 HDR/环境光与天体直射，但不能关掉隧道基础照明。普通桥底动态聚光同样只属于 High，并仍仅在弱太阳且 `underpassBlend > 0.68` 时渐亮，合计照度不超过太阳的 `32%`，晴朗白天关闭。

## English

Owns procedural modeling and physical lighting. This layer controls materials, HDR, real lights, and shadows, but never gameplay authority.

Before road-pool construction, the rig installs `scene.userData.neonTunnelLightingUniforms`: three stable uniforms carrying actual ambient, cached-HDR, and directional indoor/outdoor intensity ratios. Road, shell, and rib receivers share these values read-only, dividing local target transmission by existing global transmission and capping the multiplier at `1`. This avoids double-darkening interiors without brightening outdoor surfaces. Disabled lighting resets the ratios to `1`; disposal restores only the scene slot still owned by this rig. No per-frame material or shader rebuild is needed.

Physical-lighting contract v13 fixes the Three r160 cache path that ignores `needsPMREMUpdate` for ordinary `DataTexture` sources. Only a change to the existing `0.025` quantized environment signature sends a `dispose` lifecycle event on the rig's exclusively owned HDR source, releasing its old source/PMREM GPU cache. `needsUpdate` then refreshes the same Float storage, and the next actual render lazily filters the new PMREM. Stable environments, repeated paused renders, and disabled lighting do not refilter. `environmentCacheInvalidationCount` counts invalidations rather than actual GPU builds. The `64×32` Float source, linear filters, authored radiance, exposure, light counts, shadow resolutions, and quality tiers retain their existing specifications.

Dynamic tunnel-light `update({ dtSeconds })` consumes actual frame seconds with paused time excluded. Explicit `0` freezes handoff progress; omission retains `1/60` second solely for older callers. The original `0.34 @ 60Hz` response defines exponential fade-in and a linear fade-out rate of `20.4` per second. Every outgoing edge/profile reaches zero before a replacement group can emit; the same frame's remaining time advances the incoming fade. Equal elapsed time therefore produces equal brightness at 30/60/120Hz without update-count-dependent blank frames. The six spots, two local shadow casters, real emitter positions, and complete static base illumination remain unchanged.

`lighting.js` remains the sole global lighting authority and gives High its contrast-focused exposure/HDR/ambient balance plus capability-capped `8K / 4K / 4K` shadows. `postprocessing.js` allocates 4× MSAA, depth AO, explicit-emitter Bloom, one ACES Filmic pass, and a weak vignette only for High. When MSAA is available, its composite shader no longer executes FXAA samples whose result had a constant zero weight, and private fully overwritten targets no longer receive redundant clears. Both optimizations preserve formats, sample counts, effective shader math, and final pixels. Low/Medium release all seven render targets and return to direct rendering.

Tunnel-fixture electricity is independent from nearby dynamic detail. Dense covers and diffusers span every mountain-gallery and underground route, and every resident fixture remains visibly powered. The same authored stations write bounded static irradiance into the existing road, continuous wall/ceiling shell, and rib/housing instance attributes. Low, Medium, and High consume it in the original batches, so the complete visible tunnel retains base pavement, wall, ceiling, and structural illumination with no new mesh or draw group. Shell, wall, rib, and road base materials keep `emissive = 0`. Only High enables six pooled spots for weak camera-near PBR highlights and shadows, with two shadow casters. Low/Medium return before reading the emitter list: they perform no validation, matching, or prewarming and never submit zero-power spots to the renderer. Entering High schedules two update cycles of local-shadow prewarming. Downgrading from High to Low/Medium extinguishes every spot, clears ordinary/Film identity and handoff state, and releases local `shadow.map`/`mapPass` resources within the same quality transaction. High ordinary views require the exact player edge/profile; Film chooses one camera-nearest authored edge/profile fixture cluster, never mixing crossing routes. A `24m` tail replaces the old `144m` hard cutoff. When a seventh fixture enters or Film selects another edge/profile, the old real emitter fades nearly to zero in its stable slot before that slot binds a real emitter from the new group and fades it in from zero. `constant-on` electricity no longer multiplies player approach, portal distance, or enclosure activation. Missing exact identity or an unloaded old record fails closed, with no player- or camera-relative fake emitter. Portal cover continues to attenuate outdoor HDR/ambient energy and celestial direct light, but it cannot switch base tunnel lighting. Dynamic soffit spots are High-only as well and retain their existing rule: weak sun plus `underpassBlend > 0.68`, combined illuminance at or below `32%` of the sun, and off in clear daylight.
