# Weather / 天气

## 中文

保存天气生命周期、隧道遮蔽和地表交互。天气控制器只发布共享快照，不能直接改写路线、速度、碰撞、生命或玩法随机数；`surface-weather.js` 只依据该快照维护积水、积雪、雪泥和积尘，并向运行时返回有界的接触系数。

封闭路线发布的 `weatherExposure` 只表示飞船当前位置的局部遮蔽，不会停止户外天气生命周期或清除原始雨量。普通高架主路不是封闭隧道；世界层可在桥下遮住近处雨线并保留桥外雨幕，音频层可在真正隧道内从同一原始雨量生成低通传导声。相机预览不拥有天气遮蔽权威。

地表接触使用飞船发布的不可变矩形压力足迹，将上一帧到当前帧的路线里程/横向位置作为连续线段，与每个水坑或雪斑椭圆的精确 Minkowski 和做扫掠求交。分段求解会保留轴半径相加会漏掉的斜角接触，因此即使在 `300m/s`、长帧或横向掠过时也不会穿过薄小地表；只有无遮挡露天路面，以及贴地或距地不超过 `0.58m` 的下洗范围，才产生压力接触。稳定地表单元按路线 edge 与量化里程键控，暂停时与天气运动时钟一起冻结。

积水、雪斑和路肩雪堆使用 `24m` 的路线稳定桶，不再等飞船驶近才创建。每个有效桶按路线实际采样的 `roadHalf` 在左右路肩各放一座雪堆，不再用固定 `8.4m` 半宽把窄匝道雪堆摆到道路之外。第 4 版布局把后方保留扩展到 `96m`，最差桶相位下的前视扩展到移动端至少 `1_104m`、桌面至少 `1_488m`；以保守世界参考 `45m/s` 计算分别有约 `24.53s / 33.07s` 的预见时间。成对实例仍属于同一个 InstancedMesh，只扩大实例覆盖而不增加绘制组；远端轮换发生在有效天气雾之外，开局时不存在的负里程桶会被隐藏，近端接触、稳定单元键、隧道遮蔽和形变恢复不会因视觉前视而改变。

积水本体使用中性、非金属的介电水：一次生成的岸边羽化、中心/浅岸粗糙度与交叉微波法线贴图让路面从湿膜自然过渡到镜面水心；路线桶的稳定旋转与轻微色差打散重复轮廓，窄路还会按真实 `roadHalf` 把水坑收回边界。水片基础抬升仅 `3mm`，波面运动后仍保持 `1.5–4.5mm` 路面间隙；雪片基础抬升 `4mm`，最大压实下移 `2.5mm`，因此不再出现厘米级悬浮或穿入路面。飞船命中后不再让整张水片厘米级上下“呼吸”，而以毫米级波面和有界横向扩散表达压力；波纹、V 形尾流和水滴也改为中性普通透明混合，不会在暗处发出青色加色光。这些变化复用既有实例、波纹、尾流和粒子池，新增绘制组为 `0`。局部能量同时读取本帧扫掠覆盖、纵向/横向浸入、积水深度、下洗压力、速度和着陆能量：擦边可以只留下轻微位移而不喷水；达到阈值后分别检查左右尾部下洗点是否真的落在水坑椭圆内，只在命中侧生成小水柱，若两尾都未浸入则仅在真实足迹接触边缘生成一束。每次接触最多 `12` 粒、每束最多 `8` 粒，水位移与波能分别按 `1.8s / 0.92s` 的时间常数回流。命中路面雪层会压实并压暗覆盖、清出双侧下洗沟槽，把雪推成左右雪脊并扬起粉雪；飞船靠近、擦过或以 `0.58m` 内的下洗扫到路肩雪堆时，同一连续足迹求交会在实际接触边缘局部降低、压实并向路外推开雪堆，同时复用既有雪沟、雪脊和粉雪池。新降雪会更快回填压实、清沟和推移，干燥期则保留更久。雪泥同时保留液体阻力与湿雪压实特征。全部变形状态限制在 `[0,1]`，恢复与 `30 / 60 / 120Hz` 渲染频率无关。

高反照率薄雪片用实体物理材质接收真实光影，但不进入默认不透明阴影深度通道；只有体积路肩雪堆投影，从而避免透明片形成黑色坑洞。路肩雪堆使用封闭的有机半丘和真实平底，局部高度严格为 `0–1`，底面仅沿道路法线抬升 `2mm`；压实只缩短丘高并保持平底锚定，不再把完整球体的下半部埋入道路。高画质保留积水、雪片/雪堆、双沟/雪脊、涟漪、V 尾流和两类接触粒子的全部九组；移动画质把雪脊编码进沟槽边缘、按速度在涟漪与尾流中二选一，并在混合态只显示当前占优粒子层，使真实参与绘制的地表组不超过六组。

地表模块不直接改写玩法。它只计算水、雪和雪泥接触强度、最大 `1.2m/s²` 的局部纵向压力减速度，以及有下限的横向加速度、阻尼和最高横移速度倍率；主运行时是唯一速度与操控写入者，并让手动驾驶、自动领航和预测读取同一份上一帧响应。水/雪速度参考分别为 `160 / 120km/h`；确定性 `60Hz` 审计要求固定 `120km/h` 的 `60s` 平均减速度不超过 `0.9m/s²`，为 2 挡约 `3.57333m/s²` 满推进权威保留约 `75%`，饱和局部载荷也不能压倒驱动力。`300m/s × 45ms` 仍作为几何扫掠压力案例，不再作为正常驾驶目标。山体廊道与地下隧道没有积水、积雪、尾流、雪痕或接触载荷，飞船高于下洗范围时也恢复为干地单位响应。

同一接触快照驱动一份复用的程序化地表声源：清水使用随速度展开的宽频水花，干雪叠加低中频压实与高频脆响，雪泥保留压实但压暗水花并削弱脆响。`getSnapshot()` 与运行时诊断公开接触类别/强度、扫掠覆盖、压力因子、减速度、横向倍率、实际前/后铺设距离、局部水柱/擦边无水花/发射粒子计数、变形单元、尾流、涟漪、雪沟和雪脊；路肩雪另公开本帧接触、累计互动、受扰雪堆单元、扫掠命中和雪堆粉雪次数，不暴露可写单元或 Three.js 池对象。

降雨期间，积水模块按暂停时钟复用同一波纹池，在露天水坑内确定性投放稀疏雨点环；它不创建新对象、不增加绘制组，也不产生玩法接触。

## English

Owns weather lifecycle, tunnel shelter, and surface interaction. The weather controller publishes one shared snapshot and cannot directly write routing, speed, collision, lives, or gameplay RNG. `surface-weather.js` uses only that snapshot to maintain water, snow, slush, and dust, then returns bounded contact coefficients to runtime.

`weatherExposure` from a sealed route describes only local shelter at the ship; it never stops the outdoor weather lifecycle or clears raw rainfall. An ordinary elevated main road is not a sealed tunnel. World may mask nearby drops under that bridge while retaining the exterior curtain, and audio may derive a low-pass tunnel return from the same raw rainfall inside a real tunnel. Camera preview has no shelter authority.

Surface contact continuously intersects the previous-to-current route-distance/lateral segment with the exact Minkowski sum of each puddle/snow ellipse and the ship's immutable rectangular pressure footprint. Piecewise solving preserves diagonal-corner contact missed by simple axis-radius addition, so thin surfaces cannot be skipped at `300m/s`, during a long frame, or during a lateral crossing. Pressure contact is limited to unobstructed open sky and to a grounded craft or downwash clearance no greater than `0.58m`. Stable surface cells are keyed by route edge and quantized station, and pause freezes them with the weather-motion clock.

Puddles, snow sheets, and shoulder banks use route-stable `24m` buckets instead of waiting until the ship is nearby. Every valid bucket places one bank on each shoulder from the route's sampled `roadHalf`; narrow ramps no longer inherit a fixed `8.4m` half-width that leaves banks detached from the road. Layout v4 retains `96m` behind, while its phase-safe forward preview reaches at least `1_104m` on mobile and `1_488m` on desktop. At the conservative `45m/s` world reference, that is approximately `24.53s / 33.07s` of warning. Both banks remain instances of the same InstancedMesh, extending coverage without adding draw groups. Recycling occurs beyond the effective weather fog, nonexistent negative-distance startup buckets stay hidden, and near contact, stable cell keys, tunnel shelter, and deformation recovery remain unchanged.

Standing water uses a neutral, non-metallic dielectric surface. One-time procedural shoreline alpha, centre-to-shore roughness, and crossed micro-normal maps blend the wet road into a reflective water centre. Stable route-bucket rotation and slight tint variation break repeated silhouettes, while the sampled `roadHalf` keeps puddles inside narrow branches. The water sheet starts only `3mm` above the road and remains within `1.5–4.5mm` after wave motion; snow starts at `4mm` and its maximum `2.5mm` compaction depression cannot enter the road. Contact no longer moves the whole sheet by centimetres: millimetre waves and bounded outward spread carry the pressure response. Ripples, V wakes, and droplets use neutral normal alpha blending instead of cyan additive glow, while reusing the existing instance, wave, wake, and particle pools with `0` added draw groups. Local energy still combines swept-frame coverage, longitudinal/lateral penetration, water depth, downwash pressure, speed, and landing energy. A graze may disturb water without emitting spray. Above threshold, left and right tail-downwash points are tested separately against the actual puddle ellipse, so only immersed sides emit a small jet; if neither tail is immersed, one jet is placed at the real footprint contact edge. Each contact emits at most `12` particles and each jet at most `8`; water displacement and wave energy return with `1.8s / 0.92s` time constants. Road-sheet snow contact compacts and darkens the cover, clears paired downwash grooves, pushes material into left/right ridges, and emits powder. When the craft approaches, grazes, or sweeps a shoulder bank from within the `0.58m` downwash range, the same continuous footprint solver locally lowers and compacts that bank, displaces it outward, and reuses the existing track, ridge, and powder pools at the actual contact edge. Fresh snowfall refills compaction, grooves, and displacement faster, while dry conditions preserve them longer. Slush retains both fluid load and wet-snow compaction. Every deformation channel is bounded to `[0,1]`, and recovery is invariant across `30 / 60 / 120Hz`.

High-albedo snow sheets use opaque physical material that receives real lighting but does not enter the default opaque shadow-depth pass; only volumetric shoulder banks cast, preventing translucent sheets from becoming black pits. Each shoulder bank is now a sealed organic half-hill with a real planar base and strict local `0–1` height. Its base sits only `2mm` along the sampled road normal, and compaction shortens the hill without moving that base, so no lower sphere remains buried in the road. High quality retains all nine surface groups for accumulation, grooves/ridges, waves/wakes, and both contact-particle layers. Mobile encodes ridge contrast into groove edges, chooses ripple or wake by speed, and shows only the dominant mixed particle layer, capping actual participating surface groups at six.

The surface module never writes gameplay state. It computes water/snow/slush contact, at most `1.2m/s²` of local longitudinal pressure deceleration, and lower-bounded lateral-acceleration, damping, and top-lateral-speed multipliers. Main runtime is the sole velocity and handling writer; manual flight, autopilot, and prediction consume the same prior-frame response. Water/snow speed references are `160 / 120km/h`. Deterministic `60Hz` audits cap the fixed-`120km/h` `60s` average at `0.9m/s²`, reserving roughly `75%` of gear 2's approximately `3.57333m/s²` full-thrust authority; even saturated local load cannot overpower drive. The `300m/s × 45ms` case remains a geometry-sweep stress test rather than a normal driving target. Mountain galleries and underground tunnels own no puddles, snow, wakes, tracks, or contact load, and flight above downwash range resolves to the dry identity response.

The same contact snapshot drives one reusable procedural surface-audio source: clear water opens into a speed-shaped broad spray, dry snow layers low-mid compression with high-frequency crunch, and slush preserves compression while darkening spray and damping brittle crunch. `getSnapshot()` and runtime diagnostics expose contact kind/intensity, swept coverage, pressure factor, deceleration, lateral multipliers, actual forward/behind layout distances, local-jet/grazing-no-spray/emitted-particle counts, deformation cells, wakes, ripples, grooves, and ridges. Shoulder snow additionally exposes current-frame contact, cumulative interactions, disturbed-bank cells, swept hits, and bank-powder bursts without exposing writable cells or pooled Three.js objects.

During rain, the surface module reuses the same ripple pool to deterministically place sparse impact rings inside open puddles from the pausable clock. It creates no new objects or draw groups and produces no gameplay contact.

## 户外气溶胶能见度 / Open-air aerosol visibility

`OPEN_AIR_VISIBILITY_CONTRACT` 把天气配置中的 `visibility` 作为基础，再连续叠加归一化 `fog` 与 `dust` 的消光贡献，使雾、烟尘和沙暴的实际户外能见度随浓度自然降低。强雾时户外近雾上限由 `18m` 收紧到 `4m`；隧道遮蔽仍使用同一连续暴露量，把雾、尘和消光平滑归一，并在完全封闭处恢复中性可见度。该合同只发布呈现快照，不改天气选择、暂停时钟、地表积累或玩法状态。

`OPEN_AIR_VISIBILITY_CONTRACT` starts from the authored `visibility` value and continuously compounds normalized `fog` and `dust` extinction, so effective outdoor visibility falls naturally with mist, smoke, and sandstorm concentration. The outdoor near-fog cap tightens from `18m` to `4m` in dense conditions. Tunnel shelter still uses the same continuous exposure value to normalize fog, dust, and extinction smoothly, restoring neutral visibility when fully enclosed. This contract publishes presentation state only and cannot change weather selection, the pausable clock, surface accumulation, or gameplay state.
