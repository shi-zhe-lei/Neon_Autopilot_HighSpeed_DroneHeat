# Gameplay / 玩法核心

## 中文

保存浏览器与 Node 共用的确定性碰撞、计时、生成和操控纯合同。不得依赖 DOM 或渲染帧率。

`PROPULSION_CORE_CONTRACT` 发布零烛光基线 `1_800–12_000 RPM`、`2.4s⁻¹` 升转，以及成长上界 `15_000 RPM / 3.6s⁻¹`；`1.8s⁻¹` 降转保持固定。`DRIVE_TRANSMISSION_CONTRACT` 把 `manual / automatic` 变速箱权限从自动驾驶和油门权限中独立出来：手动模式只接受玩家 Q/E 或按钮，自动模式禁用玩家 Q/E 并只接受调度器的 `1↔2↔3` 请求；3 挡是自动挡同样可用的高速道路与跳台推进级。自动调度按 `90 / 70km/h` 切换1、2挡，并按 `140 / 120km/h` 切换2、3挡，两组 `20km/h` 滞回避免临界速度反复换挡。P 的 `120km/h` 普通巡航通常保持2挡，P 关闭后的无目标上限满油门可自动升入3挡。`DRIVE_GEAR_CONTRACT` 发布三段真实推进力，干燥平路正常工作区满油门分别以约 `1.95875 / 3.57333 / 9.57333m/s²` 推进，并由同一阻力方程自然平衡在约 `110 / 160 / 280km/h`，不是硬限速。仪表推荐带为 1 挡 `0–90km/h`、2 挡 `70–140km/h`、3 挡 `140–280km/h`，与前述自然平衡速度是不同概念；2 挡低于 `70km/h`、3 挡低于 `140km/h` 时，`resolveDriveGearTorqueAvailability(...)` 先发布随传动速度线性下降的机械转速耦合 `n`，再以 `n²` 快速削减可用推进，分别在 `35 / 90km/h` 完全失速归零。推进 spool 仍只追随油门，实际推力只乘一次 `n²`；加载后的 RPM 目标改为 `油门×n`，因此高挡低速即使持续踩油门也会真实掉转，而不会在零推力时维持红线。解析结果同时给出拖挡严重度、完全失速、建议恢复挡位与所需连续降挡次数，但不叠加第二份虚假纵向阻力。MT 的 Q/E 只请求相邻挡；升入 2/3 挡至少需要 `70 / 140km/h`，降入 1/2 挡分别不得高于 `125 / 190km/h`，拒绝换挡不会改写速度。每次接受换挡都有精确 `0.30s` 断扭和 RPM 回落。`resolveCandleHandling(...)` 对已收集数量 `c` 计算共享进度 `p=c/(c+18)`，由 `maximumRpm=12_000×(1+0.25p)` 和 `spoolUpRatePerSecond=2.4×(1+0.50p)` 连续提高当前 RPM 上限与升转率，并把同一 `p` 作为飞船“暖金 → 紫 → 青”调色进度。调用方可复用输出对象，不产生每帧结果分配。

`resolveAutomaticDriveGearDecision(...)` 是自动 `1↔2` 的纯调度权威：在 `90km/h` 升挡、`70km/h` 降挡，保留 `20km/h` 防抖区；手动模式、换挡过程和 3 挡一律保持当前挡。升挡点远离 1 挡 `110km/h` 渐近平衡尾段，因此从静止自动起步不会在换挡前拖延；`120Hz` 回归要求约 `20–35s` 到 `115km/h`、`40s` 内到 `119km/h`，且只发生一次 `1→2`。

`LONGITUDINAL_DYNAMICS_CONTRACT` 是唯一纵向速度合同。`resolveLongitudinalResistance(...)` 把接地滚阻 `0.24m/s²`、线性阻力 `0.015v` 与空气阻力 `0.001_35v²` 合成为被动载荷；`resolveLongitudinalDynamicsStep(...)` 再以解析积分统一处理推进、`7.5m/s²` 行车制动、地表载荷和坡度。新局从真实 `0` 起步；松开 W 会立即切掉踏板额外推力，核心余转仍按 `1.8s⁻¹` 回落。独立的 `IDLE_CREEP_CONTRACT` 只在贴地、1挡接合、零踏板、未制动且未断扭时提供真实液力耦合力：干燥平路约 `8km/h` 是怠速力与同一阻力的平衡点，`16km/h` 起怠速力严格归零；它不是速度下限，坡度与地表可让飞船低于该值甚至停住。S 能完全停车并持续压住怠速，换挡、腾空和2/3挡都不获得隐藏蠕行力。三个满油门干地平衡点同样来自推进力与同一阻力的交点；天气、坡度与腾空状态可改变实际平衡速度。`resolveImpactSpeed(...)` 让有效护栏、静态障碍和动态障碍碰撞分别保留 `78% / 58% / 72%` 纵向速度，不再让撞击保持原速度，也不会让碰撞增加动能。

`STEERING_ACTUATOR_CONTRACT` 固定 `5.5/s` 接合、`7.5/s` 回中和 `8.0/s` 反向消除速率。`advanceSteeringActuator(...)` 让手动与自动驾驶共享同一渐进执行器：反向输入先以反向速率消除当前偏转，再以接合速率进入另一侧；函数同时返回 `nextActuator` 与精确的 `averageActuator`，因此跨越中位或目标值的帧也不会因 `30/60/120Hz` 分片不同而改变转向积分。

`DAMAGE_PROTECTION_CONTRACT` 深冻结普通伤害保护：无敌时长固定为 `1.32s` 活动玩法时间、明显冲击反馈时长为 `0.48s`、同一渲染帧最多接受一次损伤，受保护尝试的玩法副作用固定为 `none`，呈现语义固定为“一次冲击、稳定护盾、船体不闪烁”。`canAcceptOrdinaryDamage(...)` 是对剩余无敌时间和本帧接受锁的纯判定；`advanceDamageInvulnerability(...)` 只扣除调用方明确传入的活动玩法秒数，因此暂停可传 `0` 并完整冻结保护期。纯核心不写生命、事件、音效、实体或 DOM；运行时必须在任何下游副作用前原子开启计时器与帧锁。

`sweptEllipseInterval(...)` 在路线局部的相对 `distance/lateral` 空间解析飞船线段与“地表椭圆 ⊕ 飞船轴对齐矩形足迹”的精确闭合接触区间。求解器在四条足迹 slab 边界处分段，再把每段化为直线与单位圆求交，因此不会漏掉简单轴半径相加会遗漏的斜角接触；切线算接触，调用方可复用 `out`，非有限、负半径或无定义退化输入安全返回未命中。`SWEPT_ELLIPSE_INTERVAL_CONTRACT` 冻结并公开这些跨模块约定。

`sweptVerticalContact(input, sampleVerticalProfile, out)` 在帧内沿路线与时间共同细分恒重力弹道。兼容合同保留 `0.5m` 路线粗上限，实际可碰撞剖面使用不超过 `0.05m` 的自适应探针，时间间隔不超过 `1/240s`；因此 `0.10m` 的短顶板也不能藏在两个采样端点之间。采样器以 `sampleVerticalProfile(distanceM, fraction, sampleOut)` 返回或原位填写世界高度 `{ surface, ceiling }`，其中 `Infinity` 表示开放天空。线性高度只用于估算候选时刻；最终接触会针对真实采样谓词二分到命中侧，所以有限高度突然下降时不能错误发布较高的安全侧。`Infinity ↔ 有限` 边界同样细化，同一时刻顶棚优先于地面。

命中结果把“首次接触证据”与“本帧结束状态”分开：`kind/fraction/time/altitude/velocity/surface/ceiling` 固定首次接触顺序，`endAltitude/endVelocity/endSurface/endCeiling/endGrounded` 则在非弹性响应后继续推进剩余时间并逐段重新约束地面和顶棚；后续落地还公开 `endGroundedFraction/endImpactVelocity`。`penetration` 描述首次重叠，`overshoot` 覆盖原始弹道在整段余量中的最大越界。未命中时两组步末值一致。`SWEPT_VERTICAL_CONTACT_CONTRACT` 冻结这些约定，调用方可复用 `out` 和 `sampleOut`，避免高速飞行每次采样分配对象。

贴地起飞是闭合边界的特殊情况：飞船带正向分离速度离开地面或顶棚时，`t=0` 根只表示上一时刻的接触历史，不能再次触发碰撞；静止、下降、相切或已经穿透仍必须在 `t=0` 立即接触。手工跳跃使用标准重力 `9.81m/s²`，由目标高度反算冲量；回归在 `22 / 30 / 60 / 120Hz` 锁定首帧离地、约 `4.6m` 峰值与约 `1.94s` 总腾空时间，并保留静止和下降接地。

`consumeBoundedElapsedTime(...)` 完整消费每个有限、可见的墙钟帧，并拆为不超过 `45ms` 的顺序玩法子步；它不再设置总追赶上限。运行时在页面隐藏时自动暂停并重置帧时钟，因此恢复后不会追赶后台时段。`105 km/h = 29.1667m/s`，Node 回归锁定 `1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 10 / 30 / 60fps` 下十秒均前进 `291.6667m`。该纯核心不创建中央动态路线亮线、光点或目标环；呈现层仅保留世界固定的米制短标、道路箭头、出口牌与路线图。

## English

Owns deterministic collision, timing, spawning, and handling contracts shared by the browser and Node. It must not depend on the DOM or render cadence.

`PROPULSION_CORE_CONTRACT` publishes the zero-candle `1_800–12_000 RPM` and `2.4s⁻¹` spool-up baseline plus the `15_000 RPM / 3.6s⁻¹` growth ceilings; spool-down remains fixed at `1.8s⁻¹`. `DRIVE_TRANSMISSION_CONTRACT` separates `manual / automatic` gearbox authority from steering autopilot and throttle authority: manual mode accepts only player Q/E or button requests, while automatic mode disables player Q/E and accepts only scheduler-owned `1↔2↔3` requests. Gear 3 is the high-performance road and ramp stage available to both modes. The automatic schedule shifts between Gears 1/2 at `90 / 70km/h` and between Gears 2/3 at `140 / 120km/h`; both `20km/h` hysteresis bands prevent threshold chatter. P's ordinary `120km/h` cruise normally remains in Gear 2, while uncapped full throttle with P off can automatically reach Gear 3. `DRIVE_GEAR_CONTRACT` publishes three real drive-thrust stages. In their normal ranges, dry-level full throttle supplies approximately `1.95875 / 3.57333 / 9.57333m/s²`, naturally balancing the same resistance equation near `110 / 160 / 280km/h` rather than clamping speed. Dashboard recommended bands are Gear 1 `0–90km/h`, Gear 2 `70–140km/h`, and Gear 3 `140–280km/h`; these are distinct from the natural equilibrium speeds above. Below `70km/h` in Gear 2 or `140km/h` in Gear 3, `resolveDriveGearTorqueAvailability(...)` first publishes a linear driveline-speed coupling `n`, then rapidly reduces usable propulsion as `n²`, reaching complete stall at `35 / 90km/h`. The thrust spool still follows the pedal and delivered thrust multiplies `n²` exactly once, while loaded RPM targets `throttle×n`; full throttle in an under-speed tall gear therefore pulls RPM down instead of showing redline at zero thrust. The result also publishes lug severity, complete stall, a recommended recovery gear, and the required number of consecutive downshifts without inventing a second longitudinal drag force. MT Q/E requests adjacent gears only. Engaging Gears 2/3 requires at least `70 / 140km/h`, while downshifting into Gears 1/2 is rejected above `125 / 190km/h`; rejection never writes speed. Every accepted shift owns an exact `0.30s` torque interruption and RPM fall. For collected count `c`, `resolveCandleHandling(...)` derives the shared `p=c/(c+18)` profile, continuously raising the current ceiling through `maximumRpm=12_000×(1+0.25p)` and spool-up through `spoolUpRatePerSecond=2.4×(1+0.50p)`, while exposing the same `p` for the ship's warm-gold → violet → cyan color progression. Callers may reuse the output object without allocating a result each frame.

`resolveAutomaticDriveGearDecision(...)` is the pure scheduling authority for automatic `1↔2`: it upshifts at `90km/h` and downshifts at `70km/h`, retaining a `20km/h` anti-chatter band; manual mode, an active shift, and gear 3 always hold the current gear. The upshift stays clear of gear 1's asymptotic `110km/h` tail so a standing automatic launch does not stall before shifting. The `120Hz` regression requires roughly `20–35s` to reach `115km/h`, less than `40s` to reach `119km/h`, and exactly one `1→2` transition.

`LONGITUDINAL_DYNAMICS_CONTRACT` is the sole speed contract. `resolveLongitudinalResistance(...)` combines `0.24m/s²` grounded rolling resistance, `0.015v` linear loss, and `0.001_35v²` aerodynamic drag. `resolveLongitudinalDynamicsStep(...)` then analytically integrates propulsion, the `7.5m/s²` service brake, surface load, and grade. Every run starts at true zero. Releasing W immediately removes excess pedal thrust while authoritative core rotation still decays at `1.8s⁻¹`. The separate `IDLE_CREEP_CONTRACT` supplies real torque-converter force only with grounded Gear 1 engaged, zero pedal, no brake, and no torque interruption: about `8km/h` is the dry-level force balance, and idle force is strictly zero from `16km/h`. It is not a speed floor—grade and surface load may hold the craft below that value or stop it. S stops and holds completely; shifts, airborne motion, and Gears 2/3 receive no hidden creep. The three full-throttle dry equilibria are likewise intersections of stage thrust and this same resistance, while weather, grade, and airborne state may change the actual balance. `resolveImpactSpeed(...)` retains `78% / 58% / 72%` of longitudinal speed for accepted guardrail, static-hazard, and moving-hazard impacts respectively; a collision can neither preserve all speed nor add kinetic energy.

`STEERING_ACTUATOR_CONTRACT` fixes `5.5/s` engagement, `7.5/s` centering, and `8.0/s` reverse-cancellation rates. `advanceSteeringActuator(...)` gives manual control and autopilot the same progressive actuator: opposite input first cancels current deflection at the reverse rate, then engages the other side. It returns both `nextActuator` and the exact `averageActuator`, so frames that cross neutral or reach a target retain the same steering integral under `30/60/120Hz` partitioning.

`DAMAGE_PROTECTION_CONTRACT` deeply freezes ordinary-damage protection: `1.32s` of active-gameplay invulnerability, `0.48s` of prominent impact feedback, at most one accepted hit per render frame, `none` as the gameplay side effects of a protected attempt, and the “single impact, steady shield, no craft blink” presentation. `canAcceptOrdinaryDamage(...)` is the pure predicate over remaining protection and the current-frame latch. `advanceDamageInvulnerability(...)` subtracts only the active-gameplay seconds explicitly supplied by its caller, so passing `0` during pause freezes the window exactly. The pure core writes no lives, events, audio, entities, or DOM; runtime must atomically arm the timer and frame latch before any downstream side effect.

`sweptEllipseInterval(...)` analytically resolves the closed contact interval between a ship segment and the exact Minkowski sum of a surface ellipse plus the ship's axis-aligned rectangular footprint in relative route-local `distance/lateral` space. It splits at the four footprint-slab boundaries and reduces each region to a line/unit-circle query, preserving diagonal-corner contact missed by simple axis-radius addition. Tangency is inclusive, callers may reuse `out`, and non-finite, negative-radius, or undefined degenerate geometry safely misses. `SWEPT_ELLIPSE_INTERVAL_CONTRACT` freezes and publishes these cross-module rules.

`sweptVerticalContact(input, sampleVerticalProfile, out)` jointly partitions a constant-gravity frame trajectory by route distance and time. The compatibility contract retains the `0.5m` coarse route ceiling, while collidable profiles use adaptive probes no farther than `0.05m` apart and time steps no larger than `1/240s`; a `0.10m` roof therefore cannot hide between endpoint samples. `sampleVerticalProfile(distanceM, fraction, sampleOut)` returns or fills world-height `{ surface, ceiling }`, with `Infinity` representing open sky. Linear heights only estimate a candidate time. The final contact is bisected against the sampler's actual predicate onto the hit side, so a finite-height drop cannot publish the higher safe side. `Infinity ↔ finite` boundaries are refined as well, and a ceiling wins an equal-time tie with the floor.

A hit separates first-contact evidence from constrained frame-end state. `kind/fraction/time/altitude/velocity/surface/ceiling` preserve chronology, while `endAltitude/endVelocity/endSurface/endCeiling/endGrounded` continue through the unused time after an inelastic response and reapply floor/ceiling constraints on every remainder interval. A later landing also publishes `endGroundedFraction/endImpactVelocity`. `penetration` describes initial overlap, while `overshoot` covers the maximum unconstrained violation across the complete remainder. The two end-state views coincide on a miss. `SWEPT_VERTICAL_CONTACT_CONTRACT` freezes these rules, while reusable `out` and `sampleOut` storage avoid per-sample allocation during high-speed flight.

Launching from rest on a closed boundary is a special case. When the ship has positive separating velocity away from a floor or ceiling, the `t=0` root is prior-contact history and must not trigger another collision; resting, descending, tangent, or already penetrating bodies still contact immediately at `t=0`. Manual launch uses standard `9.81m/s²` gravity and derives impulse from the target height. Regression at `22 / 30 / 60 / 120Hz` locks first-frame takeoff, the approximately `4.6m` apex, and roughly `1.94s` total airtime while retaining resting and descending floor contact.

`consumeBoundedElapsedTime(...)` consumes every finite visible wall-clock frame in full and partitions it into ordered gameplay substeps no larger than `45ms`; there is no total catch-up cap. Runtime auto-pauses and resets frame timing when the document becomes hidden, so resume never catches up the background interval. `105 km/h = 29.1667m/s`, and Node regression locks `291.6667m` over ten seconds at `1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 10 / 30 / 60fps`. This pure core creates no moving center-route glow line, dots, or target ring; presentation retains only world-fixed metre marks, road arrows, exit signs, and the route map.

## 可解释飞行评级 / Explainable flight rating

`FLIGHT_RATING_CONTRACT` 与 `resolveFlightRating(...)` 是结算页唯一评分权威。纯函数从整局距离、暂停外时长、最高速度、有效扣血及其护栏子类、路外坠毁、烛光、起飞/落地和分项腾空累计量生成深冻结快照；输入不会被修改，零值或非有限值不会产生 `NaN`。安全分使用 `distanceKm / (damageCount + 1)` 的平均无损航段，因此第一次真实扣血也一定降低同航程安全分；`guardrailDamageCount` 被限制为总扣血的子集，`obstacleDamageCount` 由两者差值生成，同一次护栏扣血不会被罚两次。安全、速度、耐力、收集、腾空的基础权重为 `30 / 25 / 15 / 15 / 15`；无可信样本的收集或腾空项被排除并按有效权重重分。平均速度严格为 `distance / elapsed`，速度项比较约 `120km/h` 自动巡航基准且不额外奖励超过基准；失败腾空保留在事实中，但腾空项只给真实道路安全落地率和成功跳台秒数计分。短局与不完整样本使用公开封顶，最终 `S / A / B / C / D` 边界固定为 `90 / 80 / 70 / 60`。该合同明确是游戏工程指数，不是现实能力或玩家分布推断。

`FLIGHT_RATING_CONTRACT` and `resolveFlightRating(...)` are the sole scoring authority for the debrief. The pure function consumes full-run distance, unpaused duration, measured maximum speed, accepted life-loss hits and their guardrail subset, off-road crashes, candlelights, takeoff/landing counts, and separated airtime accumulators, then returns a deeply frozen snapshot without mutating input or publishing `NaN` for empty/non-finite evidence. Safety uses the average damage-free segment `distanceKm / (damageCount + 1)`, so the first accepted hit must lower safety for an otherwise identical run. `guardrailDamageCount` is clamped to the total and `obstacleDamageCount` is their difference, preventing a guardrail hit from being penalized twice. Safety, pace, endurance, collection, and airborne performance have base weights `30 / 25 / 15 / 15 / 15`; collection or airborne dimensions without defensible samples are excluded and valid weights are renormalized. Average speed is exactly `distance / elapsed`, pace compares against the roughly `120km/h` P-Autopilot cruise reference without extra credit above it, and failed airtime remains visible without earning safe-flight credit. Published short-run and incomplete-sample caps precede fixed `S / A / B / C / D` boundaries at `90 / 80 / 70 / 60`. This contract is explicitly an in-game engineering index, not an inference about real-world ability or player distribution.
